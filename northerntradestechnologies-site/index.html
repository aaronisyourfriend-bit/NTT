Northern Trades Technologies, LLC makes simple, affordable software and hardware for electrical, construction and trade contractors, starting with safety-meeting compliance.

Who we are

We've run a contracting business ourselves, so we know how much of a supervisor's week goes to work that has nothing to do with the craft: tracking safety meetings, documenting toolbox talks for insurers and regulators, and keeping up with paperwork.

Northern Trades Technologies exists to give that time back, one workflow at a time. We're based in Trapper Creek, Alaska, and we build for contractors everywhere.
